About Bharani Subramanian

Bharani Subramanian is a dedicated traditional astrologer with over 7 years of experience in the field. He is well-versed in Tamil astrology and has studied various gurus, including experts in traditional astrology, Nadi astrology and numerology.

His practice is rooted in the belief that astrology is a powerful tool to bring clarity, guidance and peace of mind to those facing life's challenges.

He is committed to helping people find solutions to their problems through authentic and practical astrological guidance.
